Best Ollama alternatives for Mac and iPhone in 2026

Updated 20 October 2026 · Data checked on the App Store on 20 October 2026

Disclosure: Pinku is made by Saga Labs, the company behind Reditor. It is listed on the same criteria as the others.

If Ollama feels too technical, LM Studio is the closest free alternative on a Mac: a desktop app with a model browser that runs models with llama.cpp and Apple's MLX. Jan is the open-source equivalent. If you want Ollama's engine without Ollama, use llama.cpp directly. GPT4All still works but has had no release since February 2025.

Ollama has no iPhone app. To run the same open models on an iPhone, use an App Store app: Locally AI (from the LM Studio team), Private LLM, Enclave or Pinku, all of which also have a Mac version. For which model to choose, see the guide to open-weight models.

1. LM Studio

Best for: the easiest switch from Ollama on a Mac, with a graphical model browser.

  • Runs models with llama.cpp and MLX; has a programmable API and a headless daemon (llmster) for servers.
  • The free plan runs local models; optional Bionic+ ($20 a month) adds hosted open models.
  • Intel Macs are not supported.

Limits: closed source; the cloud plans are separate from local use.

2. Jan

Best for: an open-source app with a chat window and a local API server.

  • Its documentation sizes models by memory: 8 GB up to about 3B, 16 GB up to about 7B, 32 GB up to about 13B.
  • Can also connect to cloud models from several providers.
  • 45,000 stars on GitHub.

Limits: on Intel Macs its bundled engine does not load models; no iPhone version.

3. llama.cpp

Best for: people who want the engine itself, with the most control over quantisation and settings.

  • Runs GGUF files, the format most open models are shared in.
  • The engine inside many of the apps on this page.
  • 131,827 stars on GitHub.

Limits: terminal only; you choose and download models yourself.

4. GPT4All

Best for: existing users who rely on its LocalDocs feature for chatting with files.

  • Chat with local documents (LocalDocs).
  • Last commit on GitHub on 27 May 2025.

Limits: no release in about 20 months, so newer model families may not be supported.

5. Locally AI by LM Studio

Best for: the same models on iPhone and Mac from one free app.

  • Apple MLX; no login; Data Not Collected.
  • 4.7 from 1,804 ratings in the US.

Limits: on Mac it needs macOS 26; curated models only.

App Store page

6. Private LLM - Local AI Chat

Best for: paying once for Mac and iPhone, with Siri and Shortcuts.

  • Models up to 14B on Mac; its own OmniQuant quantisation.
  • Family Sharing; Data Not Collected.

Limits: a fixed list of mostly older model families.

App Store page

7. Enclave - Local AI Assistant

Best for: documents, voice and custom assistants, with optional cloud models.

  • "Hundreds" of open models, per its description.
  • Pro adds cloud models.

Limits: requires the newest macOS and iOS (27).

App Store page

8. Pinku: Private Offline AI Chat

Best for: a short tested list that hides models your device cannot run, in 8 interface languages.

  • Chats never leave the device; Apple's privacy label lists only purchase history and an anonymous user ID, used for Pinku Pro purchases.
  • Downloads from ModelScope where Hugging Face is blocked.

Limits: 12 curated models only; the Mac version is behind the iPhone version; few ratings.

App Store page

Who should use what

  • You want Ollama, but with windows and menus: LM Studio.
  • You want that, but open source: Jan.
  • You want the engine and full control: llama.cpp.
  • You want the same thing on your iPhone: Locally AI, Private LLM, Enclave or Pinku. Compared in detail on Reviewland.

Use cases

  • Run a coding model such as Devstral Small 2 or Qwen3.5 on a Mac with 32 GB or more.
  • Keep sensitive documents off cloud services.
  • Carry a small model on your iPhone for flights and places without signal.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best Ollama alternative for Mac?

LM Studio for a free desktop app with a model browser; Jan if you want it open source; llama.cpp if you are happy in a terminal.

Is there an Ollama app for iPhone?

No. To run open models on iPhone use an App Store app such as Locally AI, Private LLM, Enclave or Pinku.

Is GPT4All still maintained?

Its latest release on GitHub is v3.10.0 from 25 February 2025 and the last commit is from 27 May 2025.

Which open model should I run?

It depends on memory. See the guide to open-weight models on aisagalab.com for sizes, licences and hardware.
