DeepSeek app for iPhone: the official app and 6 alternatives

Updated 3 November 2026 · Data checked on the App Store on 3 November 2026

Yes, DeepSeek has an official iPhone app. It is DeepSeek - AI Assistant from Hangzhou DeepSeek Artificial Intelligence Co., Ltd: free, with no in-app purchases listed, in 71 languages. On 3 November 2026 its US App Store rating was 3.9 from 12,484 ratings, and its privacy label lists Data Linked to You.

People look elsewhere for two reasons. To use DeepSeek next to other models in one chat, Chat AI, AI Chat and Ask AI name DeepSeek in their listings, beside Claude and Gemini. To keep everything on the phone, Locally AI, Private LLM and Enclave run DeepSeek-based models offline; these are small distilled versions (Private LLM names DeepSeek R1 Distill), not the full model the official app uses.

1. Chat AI: Ask Agent Anything

Best for: people who want DeepSeek V4 Pro and many other models, plus images and files, in one big app.

  • Its listing names DeepSeek V4 Pro next to GPT-6, Claude Opus 5, Gemini 3.1 Pro, Grok 4.7 and Perplexity.
  • Also makes images, reads PDF, TXT and DOCX files, searches the web with sources and has hands-free voice.
  • The most ratings of any app here (329,089).

Limits: weekly plans only, plus a lifetime option, and its privacy label includes Data Used to Track You.

App Store page

2. Locally AI by LM Studio

Best for: running a DeepSeek-based model offline, for free, with nothing collected.

  • Runs models such as Llama, Gemma, Qwen and DeepSeek on the iPhone and iPad, offline, with Apple MLX, per its listing.
  • No login and no data collection: its privacy label is Data Not Collected.
  • Made by the team behind LM Studio for the Mac and PC.

Limits: on-device models are much smaller than the DeepSeek in the official app, and answers depend on your iPhone's memory.

App Store page

3. AI Chat - Smart AI Assistant

Best for: a chat app in many languages that offers DeepSeek for coding and technical questions.

  • Its listing names Claude, DeepSeek and Gemini, and suggests DeepSeek for coding and technical tasks.
  • Searches the web, makes and edits images, and reads PDFs and other files.
  • In 38 languages.

Limits: it has not been updated since 14 August 2026, and the plans are pricier than Ask AI's.

App Store page

4. All AI Models in One: Ask AI

Best for: switching between DeepSeek, Claude, Gemini and Grok in the same conversation.

  • Its listing names models from several companies, including Gemini, Claude, Grok, DeepSeek and Perplexity Sonar, and you can switch model in the middle of a chat.
  • With Pro, it searches the web by itself when a question needs current information.
  • Reads photos you attach and can read any answer aloud.

Limits: most models and web search need Pro (a few free messages a day without it), it does not read files or make images, and it has only 3 ratings.

App Store page

5. Private LLM - Local AI Chat

Best for: paying once, with no subscription, for offline models that include DeepSeek R1 Distill.

  • Its listing names DeepSeek R1 Distill based models next to Llama, Gemma, Qwen and Mistral, all running on the device.
  • Works with Siri and Shortcuts, and lets you set your own system prompt.
  • Its privacy label is Data Not Collected.

Limits: it costs $4.99 up front, is in English only, the download is 1.36 GB, and it has the lowest rating here (4.2).

App Store page

6. Enclave - Local AI Assistant

Best for: an offline app that shows DeepSeek R1's reasoning, with optional cloud models later.

  • Runs open models on the iPhone, DeepSeek among them, and displays the reasoning of thinking models such as DeepSeek R1, per its listing.
  • Chats with PDFs, text files and images locally; voice chat works offline.
  • Pro adds cloud models from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and xAI, and web search.

Limits: it needs iOS 27, and offline models are small versions, not the full DeepSeek.

Who should use what

  • DeepSeek itself, free: the official DeepSeek app.
  • DeepSeek plus many other models, images and files: Chat AI.
  • DeepSeek offline, free, nothing collected: Locally AI.
  • DeepSeek, Claude, Gemini and Grok in the same chat: Ask AI.
  • Offline with a one-time price: Private LLM.
  • Offline now, cloud models later: Enclave.

Use cases

  • Asking DeepSeek a coding question.
  • Comparing DeepSeek's answer with Claude's or Gemini's.
  • Using a DeepSeek model with no internet.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an official DeepSeek app for iPhone?

Yes: DeepSeek - AI Assistant, from Hangzhou DeepSeek Artificial Intelligence Co., Ltd. It is free, with no in-app purchases listed on 3 November 2026.

Can I use DeepSeek on iPhone without internet?

Not with the official app. Locally AI, Private LLM and Enclave run smaller DeepSeek-based models on the phone with no connection; they are distilled versions, so answers are simpler than the full model's.

Which app lets me use DeepSeek and other models in one place?

Chat AI, AI Chat and Ask AI all name DeepSeek in their listings, next to Claude and Gemini. Ask AI lets you switch model in the middle of a conversation.

Is Chat AI the same app as Ask AI?

No. Chat AI: Ask Agent Anything is from DEEP FLOW SOFTWARE SERVICES (Codeway); its in-app purchases are named «Ask AI Premium». All AI Models in One: Ask AI is a different app, from Saga Ltd.
