Best AI UGC ad generators for small shops on iPhone

Updated 2 November 2026 · Data checked on the App Store on 2 November 2026

What matters for a small shop is what you must provide. Creatify, Zeely and Pollo AI start from a product link or URL. Kiva starts from a product photo. HeyGen starts from a short clip of you. invideo starts from an idea or footage and expects you to edit. Dora starts from a photo or description and makes one clip.

Tell viewers honestly when a person in an ad is AI, and do not invent customer reviews: in the US, the FTC rule on fake reviews and testimonials covers testimonials from people who do not exist.

1. Creatify AI UGC Video Ad Maker

Best for: a seller who wants a talking AI actor holding the real product, starting from a product link.

  • Paste a product link, TikTok Shop included, or add up to 4 product photos, per its listing.
  • One selfie makes a character you can reuse across ads, or pick one of 7 ready-made AI actors.
  • Shows character, product and template for you to confirm before credits are spent.

Limits: the credit packs start at $29.99, and the weekly plan is the second highest here.

App Store page

2. Kiva: AI Product Video & Ads

Best for: marketplace sellers (TikTok Shop, Amazon, Shopify) who start from a product photo and want many versions.

  • Turns a product photo into a script and a video with an AI spokesperson, per its listing.
  • Product-in-hand, review-style and multi-presenter formats, with new versions by hook and presenter.
  • Translates videos with AI voiceover and lip sync.

Limits: a young app, and the $279.99 yearly plan is costly if you only need a few ads.

App Store page

3. Zeely: AI Marketing Platform

Best for: small shops that want ad scripts and avatars from a product link.

  • Its listing says it pulls key information from your product link.
  • AI avatars made from real UGC creators and sales scripts.

Limits: the highest weekly price here ($19.99), and it is built around marketing, not general video.

App Store page

4. DORA · AI Video Generator

Best for: a quick product clip with the cost shown first, not a talking presenter.

  • Its listing covers product videos and ads, vertical 9:16, up to 1080p, no watermark.
  • The coin cost shows before you generate.
  • The app has a template category called UGC Hooks.

Limits: it has no ad scripts, no product-link import and no ad publishing in its listing, and no free video.

App Store page

5. AI Avatar Generator - HeyGen

Best for: a talking presenter ad without filming each version.

  • Record one short clip and it builds your avatar, per its listing.
  • Type a script and the avatar delivers it.

Limits: you need to record yourself first, and it does not generate scenes around a product.

App Store page

6. Pollo AI: AI Video & Image

Best for: sellers who also want images and design tools in the same app.

  • Takes ideas, scripts, photos, product images, URLs or reference videos, per its listing.
  • AI video and image generators and an ad maker.

Limits: credits are not cheap at the small packs, and we did not test its output.

App Store page

7. invideo: AI Video Editor

Best for: teams who will edit many versions of an ad.

  • Its listing says 200+ AI models and an agent called Agent Two.
  • A professional timeline editor with collaboration.

Limits: English only, and the most complex and costly option here.

App Store page

Who should use what

  • From a product link: Creatify, Zeely or Pollo AI.
  • From a product photo, many versions: Kiva.
  • Launching the ads from the app too: Zeely.
  • You on camera, without filming: HeyGen.
  • Many edited versions: invideo.
  • One product clip: Dora.

Use cases

  • Testing ten opening lines.
  • A product clip for a shop page.
  • A short ad for Reels.

How we checked

On 2 November 2026 we read each app's US App Store page and Apple's lookup data. What each app does comes from its own listing. We ran no quality test.

Prices change often. Check inside each app before paying.

Web-first tools such as Arcads and MakeUGC are left out: searching the US App Store for those names on 2 November 2026 showed no iPhone app from them.

Frequently asked questions

What is a UGC ad?

An ad that looks filmed by a customer, usually a person talking to a phone.

Which is cheapest to try?

Among weekly plans listed: Pollo AI $5.99, Dora $6.99 for new subscribers, Kiva $9.99, HeyGen $10, Creatify $17.99, Zeely $19.99. Costs per video differ, so compare inside the apps.

Can AI avatars pretend to be customers?

They should not. In the US, the FTC rule on fake reviews and testimonials covers testimonials from people who do not exist, so do not present an AI person as a real buyer.
