Disclosure: Dora is made by Saga Labs, the company behind Reditor. It is one more option here, not placed higher because it is ours, and it has only 16 ratings in the US. The other apps are not ours and we get nothing from them.

What matters for a small shop is what you must provide. Zeely and Pollo AI start from a product link or URL. HeyGen starts from a short clip of you. invideo starts from an idea or footage and expects you to edit. Dora starts from a photo or description and makes one clip.

Tell viewers honestly when a person in an ad is AI, and do not invent customer reviews: in the US, the FTC rule on fake reviews and testimonials covers testimonials from people who do not exist.

1. Zeely: AI Marketing Platform

Best for: small shops that want ad scripts and avatars from a product link.

  • Its listing says it pulls key information from your product link.
  • AI avatars made from real UGC creators and sales scripts.

Limits: the highest weekly price here, and it is built around marketing, not general video.

App Store page

2. AI Avatar Generator - HeyGen

Best for: a talking presenter ad without filming each version.

  • Record one short clip and it builds your avatar, per its listing.
  • Type a script and the avatar delivers it.

Limits: you need to record yourself first, and it does not generate scenes around a product.

App Store page

3. DORA · AI Video Generator

Best for: a quick product clip with the cost shown first, not a talking presenter.

  • Its listing covers product videos and ads, vertical 9:16, up to 1080p, no watermark.
  • The coin cost shows before you generate.

Limits: it has no ad scripts, no product-link import and no ad publishing in its listing, no free video, and only 16 ratings in the US.

App Store page

4. Pollo AI: AI Video & Image

Best for: sellers who also want images and design tools in the same app.

  • Takes ideas, scripts, photos, product images, URLs or reference videos, per its listing.
  • AI video and image generators and an ad maker.

Limits: credits are not cheap at the small packs, and we did not test its output.

App Store page

5. invideo: AI Video Editor

Best for: teams who will edit many versions of an ad.

  • Its listing says 200+ AI models and an agent called Agent Two.
  • A professional timeline editor with collaboration.

Limits: English only, and the most complex and costly option here.

App Store page

Who should use what

  • From a product link: Zeely or Pollo AI.
  • You on camera, without filming: HeyGen.
  • Many edited versions: invideo.
  • One product clip: Dora.

Use cases

  • Testing ten opening lines.
  • A product clip for a shop page.
  • A short ad for Reels.

Frequently asked questions

What is a UGC ad?

An ad that looks filmed by a customer, usually a person talking to a phone.

Which is cheapest to try?

Of the prices listed, Dora's 300 coins at $3.99 or Pollo AI's weekly at $5.99. Costs per video differ, so compare inside the apps.

Can AI avatars pretend to be customers?

They should not. In the US, the FTC rule on fake reviews and testimonials covers testimonials from people who do not exist, so do not present an AI person as a real buyer.
