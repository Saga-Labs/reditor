Best Blockfolio alternatives for iPhone in 2026

Updated 3 November 2026 · Data checked on the App Store on 3 November 2026

Blockfolio, the crypto portfolio tracker, is no longer on the App Store: on 3 November 2026 we found no Blockfolio or FTX app from the original company in the US store. FTX bought it in August 2020 for $150 million, and FTX has been in Chapter 11 bankruptcy since 11 November 2022, per Wikipedia. A paid App Store app called «Blockfolio Pro: Crypto Tracker» ($1.99) comes from a different developer, Roxwin Vietnam Technologies, and had one rating on 3 November 2026; it is not the original.

To track your coins the way Blockfolio did, by typing in what you hold, Bitfolio, Crypton and BitWorth need no account and list no exchange connection; BitWorth and Crypton list no purchases at all. To sync wallets and exchanges automatically, CoinStats connects 300+ of them, and AssetDash syncs up to 3 Solana wallets for free. reviewland compares more trackers in CoinStats alternatives.

1. CoinStats: Crypto Portfolio

Best for: syncing every wallet and exchange automatically, with P&L and tax export.

  • Connects 300+ wallets and exchanges (MetaMask, Phantom, Binance, Coinbase…) or a pasted wallet address.
  • Realized and unrealized profit and loss, cost basis, price alerts and perpetual futures history.
  • Prices for 20,000+ coins; exports transaction history for taxes.

Limits: the full price list is long and high (up to $164.99 a year), and the listing does not say what stays free.

App Store page

2. Crypto Tracker by BitScreener

Best for: manual entry like Blockfolio, plus exchange API and CSV import, with a cheap lifetime option.

  • Portfolio by manual entry, exchange API or CSV import and export, with 1H to 1M performance.
  • Prices from 200+ markets, a screener with 100+ criteria, alerts and news.
  • Lifetime premium is listed at $9.99.

Limits: its privacy label includes Data Used to Track You and Data Linked to You, and its last update was 29 May 2026.

App Store page

3. Bitfolio - Crypto portfolio

Best for: a private tracker with no account, several portfolios, and other assets next to crypto.

  • No account; portfolio data stays on the device, per its listing.
  • 10,000+ coins, tokens and perpetual markets; multiple portfolios and watchlists.
  • Can also track real estate, collectibles and other personal assets.

Limits: it needs iOS 26, and some features are in Pro.

App Store page

4. Crypto Tracker: Crypton

Best for: a fast, free tracker with P&L per coin and Home Screen widgets, no account needed.

  • Live prices for the top 250 coins, plus any coin listed on CoinGecko; charts from 1 day to 1 year.
  • Portfolio with profit and loss per coin from your average buy price; Home Screen widgets for prices and portfolio.
  • No purchases listed; an optional account only syncs the portfolio.

Limits: English only, no exchange or wallet sync, no price alerts in its listing, and it needs iOS 18.

App Store page

5. BitWorth - Crypto Tracker

Best for: the simplest answer to «what is my crypto worth right now», free.

  • No account, no sign-up and no exchange connections; add the coins you hold.
  • Everything stays on the device unless you choose to back it up, per its listing.
  • A live global chat and news feed; free, made by one developer.

Limits: English only, it needs iOS 26, and it is deliberately simple: no P&L or alerts named in its listing.

App Store page

6. AssetDash: Portfolio Tracker

Best for: Solana holders who want wallets, tokens and NFTs synced for free.

  • Free tier syncs up to 3 Solana wallets, with net worth and allocation.
  • Premium ($4.99 a month) adds other chains, exchanges and brokerages such as Coinbase, Binance and Robinhood.
  • Also trades Solana tokens inside the app.

Limits: anything beyond Solana needs Premium, its privacy label lists Data Linked to You, and its last update was 18 November 2025.

Who should use what

  • Every wallet and exchange synced: CoinStats.
  • Manual entry plus CSV, cheap lifetime: BitScreener.
  • No account, several portfolios: Bitfolio.
  • Free, P&L per coin and widgets: Crypton.
  • Just the total, free: BitWorth.
  • Solana wallets: AssetDash.

Use cases

  • Moving a Blockfolio-style manual portfolio to a new app.
  • Checking your total on a Home Screen widget.
  • Tracking coins without linking an exchange.

How we checked

On 3 November 2026 we searched the US App Store for «blockfolio», «crypto portfolio tracker» and «crypto tracker», and read each app's US App Store page and Apple's lookup data. Blockfolio's history comes from Wikipedia's article on FTX. We installed nothing.

None of these apps buys or sells for you except AssetDash's Solana trading; they show prices and what your holdings are worth. This page is not investment advice.

Prices change often. Check inside each app before paying.

Frequently asked questions

What happened to Blockfolio?

FTX bought it in August 2020 for $150 million. FTX filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy on 11 November 2022, per Wikipedia.

Is «Blockfolio Pro» on the App Store the original Blockfolio?

No. «Blockfolio Pro: Crypto Tracker» is a $1.99 app from Roxwin Vietnam Technologies, with one US rating on 3 November 2026.

Which Blockfolio alternative is free?

Crypton and BitWorth list no purchases. Bitfolio, BitScreener and AssetDash have free versions with paid upgrades.

Which ones work without an account or exchange connection?

Bitfolio and BitWorth need no account; Crypton's account is optional and only syncs your portfolio. All three let you type in what you hold.
