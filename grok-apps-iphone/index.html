Apps to use Grok on iPhone in 2026

Updated 3 November 2026 · Data checked on the App Store on 3 November 2026

The direct way to use Grok on iPhone is the official Grok app from X Corp. It is free to download, with SuperGrok Lite at $10 a month, SuperGrok $30, Plus $100 and Heavy $300 listed on the US App Store on 3 November 2026, and a 4.9 rating from 1,567,769 ratings. Its listing covers chat, deep research, voice mode and real-time information from X.

Six other apps also give you Grok. The X app has Ask Grok built in, for questions about posts and trending news. Chat AI, ChatNow, Ask AI, Chat Smith and Nova put a Grok model next to Claude, Gemini and others in one chat, so you can compare answers or switch without a second subscription. This page is about Grok as a chat assistant; for Grok Imagine video, see reviewland's Grok Imagine alternatives.

1. Chat AI: Ask Agent Anything

Best for: the widest choice: Grok 4.7 next to GPT-6, Claude Opus 5, Gemini and DeepSeek, plus images and files.

  • Its listing names Grok 4.7 next to GPT-6, Claude Opus 5, Gemini 3.1 Pro, DeepSeek V4 Pro and Perplexity.
  • Also makes images, reads PDF, TXT and DOCX files and searches the web with sources.
  • Its in-app purchases are named «Ask AI Premium», but it is a different app from Ask AI below.

Limits: weekly plans only, plus a lifetime option, and its privacy label includes Data Used to Track You.

App Store page

2. X

Best for: people already on X who want Grok's take on a post, a video or the day's news.

  • Ask Grok is built into the app: it summarizes trending news, explains videos and adds context to posts, per its listing.
  • Grok also powers X's photo and video editing tools.
  • From the same company as the Grok app.

Limits: it is a social network first, so Grok sits inside a feed rather than a clean chat app.

App Store page

3. AI Chat Assistant – ChatNow

Best for: asking several models the same question, Grok included, and keeping the best answer.

  • Its listing names Grok 4.5 and Grok 4.3 among more than 30 models, with GPT-6 Astra, Claude Fable 5.1 and Gemini 3.1 Pro.
  • You can ask several models the same question and compare the answers.
  • Also makes images and video with models such as Nano Banana Pro, Veo 3.1 and Seedance 2.5.

Limits: it needs iOS 26, and the plans range widely ($6.99 to $79.99 listed) without the listing saying which is which.

App Store page

4. All AI Models in One: Ask AI

Best for: switching between Grok, Claude, Gemini and DeepSeek in the same conversation.

  • Its listing names models from several companies, including Gemini, Claude, Grok, DeepSeek and Perplexity Sonar, and you can switch model in the middle of a chat.
  • With Pro, it searches the web by itself when a question needs current information.
  • Reads photos you attach and can read any answer aloud.

Limits: most models and web search need Pro (a few free messages a day without it), it does not read files or make images, and it has only 3 ratings.

App Store page

5. Chat Smith: AI Chatbot & Agent

Best for: writing tools around the chat: a humanizer, an AI detector and a logo maker.

  • Its listing names Grok-4.5 next to GPT-6 Sol, Claude Sonnet 5, Gemini 3 and DeepSeek V4.
  • Adds an AI Humanizer, an AI Detector, an AI logo maker and a calendar agent.
  • A lifetime purchase is listed at $99.99.

Limits: the download is large (725 MB), and its privacy label includes Data Used to Track You and Data Linked to You.

App Store page

6. AI Chatbot - Nova

Best for: a simple chat app with Grok, Kimi and DeepSeek, plus voice mode and file work.

  • Its listing names xAI Grok 4.5 next to GPT-5.6, Gemini 3.6 Flash, Claude Opus 5, Kimi K3 and DeepSeek V4 Pro.
  • Voice mode, web search, image generation, and work on Word and Excel files.
  • Translates in 140+ languages, per its listing.

Limits: its listing describes most features as built on OpenAI GPT-5.6, so check that Grok is selectable before paying.

Who should use what

  • Grok itself, with SuperGrok when you need more: the official Grok app.
  • The most models, plus images and files: Chat AI.
  • Grok on posts and news: X.
  • Comparing several models' answers side by side: ChatNow.
  • Grok, Claude, Gemini and DeepSeek in the same chat: Ask AI.
  • Writing extras like a humanizer: Chat Smith.

Use cases

  • Getting Grok's view on today's news.
  • Comparing Grok's answer with Claude's or Gemini's.
  • Using Grok without a SuperGrok subscription.

How we checked

On 3 November 2026 we searched the US App Store for «grok», «grok ai», «grok chat» and «ai chat multiple models», and read each app's US App Store page and Apple's lookup data. Frequently asked questions

Is there an official Grok app for iPhone?

Yes: Grok, from X Corp. It is free to download; SuperGrok plans from $10 a month were listed on 3 November 2026.

Can I use Grok inside the X app?

Yes. The X app's listing describes Ask Grok, which summarizes trending news, explains videos and adds context to posts.

Which apps let me use Grok and other models in one place?

Chat AI, ChatNow, Ask AI, Chat Smith and Nova all name Grok in their listings, next to models such as Claude and Gemini. Ask AI lets you switch model in the middle of a conversation; ChatNow lets you compare answers.
