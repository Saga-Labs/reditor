About Reditor and how we check

Reditor is run by Saga Labs, a small studio that makes iPhone apps (Photo Vault, Drop, Pinku, 3 Tasks, Crypton, CarScreen, Dora, Ask AI and Brainy). Contact: sagalabs@proton.me.

Our apps are one more option, and we say which

When one of our apps fits a question, it is included as one more option, placed where it fits rather than first, and the footer of every page names our apps. Competitors are linked to their normal App Store pages and shown with their real data, including where they are better than ours.

What we check

  • Price and in-app purchases, version and release date, rating, languages, minimum iOS: from the App Store listing (US store unless the page says otherwise) on the date shown.
  • Privacy: the App Store privacy label as published by each developer.
  • Features: only what the developer states on its App Store listing or official site. We do not invent reviews or scores.

Updates

We recheck each page about once a month. The "Updated" date only changes when the data has actually been checked again.
