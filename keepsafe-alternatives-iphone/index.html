Best Keepsafe alternatives for iPhone in 2026

Updated 3 November 2026 · Data checked on the App Store on 3 November 2026

Keepsafe is still a big, maintained photo vault: Secret Photo Vault: Keepsafe has 4.8 stars from 385,535 US ratings and was updated on 26 October 2026. People look elsewhere for three reasons its own listing shows: Keepsafe Basic is ad supported, Premium is a subscription (listed at $9.99 a month or $23.99 a year, among other prices), and its privacy label includes Data Used to Track You.

Of the seven below, Safe Lock and Photo Vault have the label Data Not Collected; Hide it Pro and 1Locker list only Data Not Linked to You. 1Locker and Photo Vault list a one-time purchase next to their subscriptions, and Hide it Pro charges only for cloud storage and a password reset. Private Photo Vault is the closest match feature for feature, with an encrypted cloud backup and a break-in report, but its label also includes tracking. 1. Private Photo Vault - Pic Safe

Best for: the closest match to Keepsafe's features, with the most ratings of any vault.

  • Password, PIN or pattern lock, password-protected albums, a decoy password and a face-down lock.
  • Break-in report with a photo and GPS location; a private web browser.
  • Cloud Vault backup encrypted with a password only you know; the free version has no cloud backup, per its listing.

Limits: its privacy label includes Data Used to Track You and Data Linked to You, and backup to its cloud needs Premium.

2. Safe Lock - Hidden Photo Vault

Best for: a vault with no data collected per its label, and backups to the cloud you already use.

  • PIN, pattern, alphabetic password, Face ID or Touch ID; shake or flip the phone to lock.
  • A fake password opens a fake gallery; break-in reports with photos; hidden camera.
  • Exports to and imports from Dropbox, Google Drive, iCloud, Box and Yandex.Disk, or over Wi-Fi.

Limits: its last update was 17 August 2026, and its listing only says the Premium subscription «unlocks all features», without listing them.

App Store page

3. Secret Photo Album · 1Locker

Best for: no ads and a one-time payment option, with passwords and notes in the same vault.

  • PIN, Face ID or Touch ID, or an alphabetic password; a decoy account hides the real content.
  • Also stores passwords, notes and contacts, and has a private browser.
  • No ads, per its listing; a one-time plan is offered next to monthly and yearly ones.

Limits: cloud backup is an in-app purchase, and it is in 5 languages.

App Store page

4. Photo Vault: Hide Pics Video

Best for: no account and no data collected, with a disguised icon and a backup file you keep yourself.

  • Face ID, Touch ID or a passcode, plus a separate password for each album.
  • Disguised icon (Calculator, Notes or Weather); flip the phone face down and it jumps to Safari, Calendar or Messages.
  • Encrypted backup to one password-locked file you keep in Files or on your computer; optional encrypted copy in your own iCloud, and the vault is left out of regular iCloud backups.

Limits: only 6 US ratings, no decoy PIN or break-in report in its listing, and unlimited photos and the iCloud copy need Premium.

App Store page

5. Hide Photos Video -Hide it Pro

Best for: hiding photos with no subscription for the basics, in an app that can pretend to crash.

  • A disguise screen makes the app look as if it crashed when someone else opens it.
  • An escape code makes the vault look empty.
  • USB export of all files in one tap; unhide back to the photo library.

Limits: English only, its last update was 11 January 2026, and resetting a forgotten password is a paid purchase ($4.99).

App Store page

6. SPV - Secret Photo Vault

Best for: photos, contacts and passwords behind one PIN, kept on the device.

  • Stores photos, videos, contacts, passwords and browser data behind a PIN.
  • Keeps nothing on any server, per its listing: everything stays in the phone's storage.
  • Weekly, monthly and yearly Premium, from $2.99 per its listing.

Limits: deleting the app deletes the photos, its label includes Data Used to Track You, it is in 4 languages, and the listing names no decoy PIN.

App Store page

7. Secret Photo Album - SA

Best for: an encrypted cloud album you can open on several devices, with break-in alerts.

  • Decoy PIN; break-in alerts with photos of intruders.
  • Encrypted cloud storage to open the album from different devices; unlimited storage with Premium.
  • A private browser with an ad blocker.

Limits: its privacy label includes Data Used to Track You and Data Linked to You, and the price list is long ($1.99 to $51.99).

Who should use what

  • Keepsafe's features, more ratings: Private Photo Vault.
  • No data collected, backup to your own Dropbox or Drive: Safe Lock.
  • One payment, no ads: 1Locker.
  • No account, disguised icon, backup file you keep: Photo Vault.
  • Free basics, app that pretends to crash: Hide it Pro.
  • Album on several devices: Secret Photo Album SA.

Use cases

  • Moving off Keepsafe without losing photos.
  • Keeping ID and card photos out of the camera roll.
  • Handing your phone to someone without showing private albums.

How we checked

On 3 November 2026 we searched the US App Store for «keepsafe», «photo vault», «private photo vault», «secret photo album» and «hide photos», and read Keepsafe's and each app's US App Store page and Apple's lookup data. We installed nothing and did not test encryption.

Before you delete Keepsafe, export your photos back to the Photos app or check that your new vault has them: several vaults, SPV for example, say photos are deleted with the app.

Prices change often. Check inside each app before paying.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free alternative to Keepsafe?

All seven are free to download. Hide it Pro sells only cloud storage and a password reset; the others limit some features to Premium. Check what the free version stores before moving everything.

Which Keepsafe alternative does not track you?

Safe Lock and Photo Vault have the App Store label Data Not Collected; Hide it Pro and 1Locker list Data Not Linked to You. Keepsafe, Private Photo Vault, SPV and Secret Photo Album SA include Data Used to Track You.

Keepsafe or Private Photo Vault?

Both are large, maintained vaults with decoy features and a cloud backup on Premium, and both labels include tracking. Private Photo Vault adds a break-in report with GPS and a private browser.

Which ones have a one-time price instead of a subscription?

1Locker («Entire Life», $24.99) and Photo Vault (lifetime $14.99 or $29.99) list one; Hide it Pro needs no subscription for its basics.
