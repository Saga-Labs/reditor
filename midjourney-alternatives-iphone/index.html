Best Midjourney alternatives for iPhone in 2026

Updated 3 November 2026 · Data checked on the App Store on 2 November 2026

There is no app called Midjourney on the iPhone. On 2 November 2026, a US App Store search for «midjourney» showed no app from Midjourney under that name; Midjourney itself is used at midjourney.com, which also opens in Safari on the iPhone. The one iPhone app from Midjourney, Inc. is niji・journey, which makes anime-style pictures and syncs with an existing niji・journey account.

For other styles, six apps do the same job natively. Leonardo.Ai and Adobe Firefly let you choose between several image models (Firefly's are trained on licensed content, per Adobe). Ideogram is known for clean text inside images, such as logos and posters. ChatGPT and Google Gemini make images inside a chat app you may already have; Gemini's listing names Nano Banana 2. Dora makes images with Nano Banana 2 too, at 10 coins an image on doravideo.com, and also makes video.

1. niji・journey - AI Anime Art

Best for: anime and illustration styles from Midjourney's own team.

  • The only iPhone app from Midjourney, Inc.; type what you want to see and pick a style.
  • A short quiz suggests a personal style, and you can remix prompts from other users.
  • Accounts and plans sync with an existing niji・journey account, per its listing.

Limits: anime style only, English only, and no free plan listed: plans start at $4.99 a month.

App Store page

2. Leonardo.Ai - Image Generator

Best for: people who want Midjourney-like control and several models in one app.

  • Its listing names Nano Banana by Google, Seedream, FLUX and Leonardo's own models.
  • Ready-made templates (Blueprints), an image editor that follows written changes, and background removal.
  • Also turns images and photos into video.

Limits: English only, and its cheapest plan ($15.99 a month) is the highest starting price here.

App Store page

3. Ideogram AI - Image Generator

Best for: logos, posters and any image that must contain readable text.

  • Free weekly credits to start, per its listing.
  • Stylized text and precise composition, a logo maker and AI headshots from one photo.
  • Generative fill, expanding to new aspect ratios and background removal.

Limits: English only, and images only: its listing names no video.

App Store page

4. DORA · AI Video Generator

Best for: someone who wants images and short videos from the same coins, with the cost shown first.

  • Images from a description or a reference photo, up to 4K, back in about a minute, per its listing; one of its image models is Nano Banana 2, at 10 coins an image on doravideo.com/models.
  • The coin cost shows before you generate, and failed generations refund the coins.
  • The same account and coins work on doravideo.com. Dora's own page compares it with Midjourney: doravideo.com/vs/midjourney.

Limits: built mainly for video, with no style quiz or community feed, and no free images: coins need a Pro plan.

App Store page

5. Adobe Firefly AI Video & Photo

Best for: work you plan to use commercially, and people who already use Adobe apps.

  • Adobe says its own Firefly models are trained on licensed content.
  • Also offers partner models from Google, OpenAI, Luma and Runway, plus video, sound and background removal.
  • In 27 languages, the most here after ChatGPT and Gemini.

Limits: credits are a separate purchase on top of the plan, and partner models use more of them.

App Store page

6. ChatGPT

Best for: people who already use ChatGPT and want to make or edit images by describing them.

  • Makes images from a description, or changes an uploaded one with a few words, per its listing.
  • The free app syncs your history across devices.

Limits: it is a chat app first, with fewer image controls than Leonardo or Midjourney.

App Store page

7. Google Gemini

Best for: people who want Google's Nano Banana 2 directly, inside an assistant.

  • Its listing names Nano Banana 2 for making and editing photos: camera and lighting controls, mockups that blend several images, posters with sharp text.
  • Google AI plans also add cloud storage (from 400 GB on Plus).

Limits: it is an assistant first, and the plans bundle storage you may not need.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a Midjourney app for iPhone?

Not under that name. Midjourney, Inc. publishes niji・journey, an anime-style image app. Midjourney itself works at midjourney.com in a browser, including Safari on the iPhone.

Is niji・journey the same as Midjourney?

It comes from the same company, Midjourney, Inc., but the app is built for anime and illustration styles and sells its own plans in the app.

Which iPhone apps use Nano Banana 2?

Of the listings we read, Google Gemini names Nano Banana 2, and Dora lists it on doravideo.com/models. Leonardo.Ai names Nano Banana by Google.

Which is cheapest to try?

Ideogram gives free weekly credits. Among paid plans listed: niji・journey Mini $4.99 a month, Firefly Mobile $4.99 a month, Google AI Plus $4.99 a month, ChatGPT Go $8 a month, Dora Pro weekly from $4.99.
